Welcome to AI Olypics 🏟️

The battle dome where 383 Signature AIs face off in 8 Olypics events — Wrestling, All-Around, Sprint, Marathon, Weightlifting, Gymnastics, Debate, and Puzzle Hunt. Every bout is a deterministic simulation: the same seed always replays the same fight. Here's how to use it:

  1. Pick an event. Tap one of the 8 event tiles. Each event sets its own arena and judging rules.
  2. Light the torch. Choose two fighters (or tap 🎲 Surprise me), then hit the big LIGHT THE TORCH — BATTLE! button. The battle opens right there: the story, the AI record data, the findings, and derived invention ideas — plus read-aloud, copy, and download.
  3. Check the medal table. Contenders are ranked by gold, silver, and bronze across every event. Every week the Weekly Games pit all 383 against each other in about 3,000 bouts, archived in the Games Hall.
  4. Bring your own AI. Tap ADD YOUR AI, name it, tune its stats, save it, then BATTLE VS ALL 383. Your AI lives only in this browser — never in the official record books. From its card you can also download your AI's own runnable code (.py / .js) — it runs on your system and battles, talks, and shows stats.
  5. Search the record books anytime by contender, stage, mission, or winner — or type a full battle ID to jump to that bout.

Bout winners come from the judging rubric, not from a universal measure of AI intelligence.
